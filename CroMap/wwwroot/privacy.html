You provide: first and last name, username, email address, phone number (optional), date of birth, password (stored only as a BCrypt hash, never in readable form), and a profile picture.

3. Legal basis

Render (hosting and database), Cloudflare R2 (media storage), Google Maps Platform (maps and place data), OpenStreetMap via Nominatim and Overpass (place search), SendGrid (email), Sentry (crash reports) and Google Sign-In (if you use it). Data is kept for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account erases it immediately and permanently — account, posts, photos and videos, messages, comments, ratings and follow lists. This cannot be undone.

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Email the address above; we respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb, azop.hr.

8. Age

VARA is not intended for people under 16. We ask for a date of birth at registration and delete accounts we learn belong to under-16s.

9. Security

Traffic between the app and the server uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored as BCrypt hashes. Access is limited by a sign-in token valid for 7 days. No measure is absolute; we will notify you and AZOP within 72 hours of a breach likely to affect your rights.
